01 · Build

Independent Store Build

Our core service. From zero, we build a store that can accept payments, run ads and keep operating. Shopify when a hosted platform fits best, WordPress/WooCommerce when you want full ownership, or a self-hosted store when needed. We recommend based on your category and budget.

For: first-time sellers opening their first export store; sellers who want to move to a new store; brands going direct-to-consumer.

What is included

  • Store creation and basic settings (Shopify / WordPress / self-hosted)
  • Theme selection and styling (desktop + mobile)
  • Home, product, about, contact and policy pages
  • Product uploads (title, description, price, stock, collections)
  • Payments setup (PayPal / credit cards)
  • Shipping and delivery-rate templates
  • Domain connection and basic email configuration
  • Basic SEO: titles, descriptions, URLs, sitemap
  • Handover guide + admin walkthrough
02 · Design

Product & Detail-page Design

Half of cross-border conversion is the image. We produce white-background shots, scene images, selling-point graphics and detail-page layouts that make your product stand out in search results.

For: sellers with weak product images, low click-through, or detail pages that look like a wholesale list.

What is included

  • Main product images (white / scene / selling-point)
  • Detail-page layouts (selling logic + visual flow)
  • Size specs for e-commerce / Amazon / DTC stores
  • Batch production (multiple angles and colors of one product)
  • When bundled with a build: images go live directly
03 · Ads

Ad Creative

Ad images, video covers, copy and landing-page suggestions. From "spending money" to "spending it right", covering social (Facebook / Instagram / TikTok) and search (Google).

For: sellers ready to advertise but without creative; sellers with poor results who need fresh creative; small budgets that must be spent carefully.

What is included

  • Ad images / video covers
  • Ad copy (multiple versions for A/B tests)
  • Landing-page suggestions for each ad
  • Platform-specific creative sizes
  • Ad structure advice (optional)
04 · Operations

Managed Operations

Launching the store is only the start. New products, pricing, customer service, orders and data reviews, managed monthly. You focus on sourcing and shipping.

For: sellers with stock but no time; sellers who operate sporadically; sellers who want the store run systematically.

What is included

  • New product uploads and removals
  • Price and stock adjustments
  • Support scripts and common-issue handling
  • Order status follow-ups
  • Monthly data review and improvement plan
  • Competitor and market updates
05 · SEO & Payments

SEO · Payments · Compliance Setup

Make the site findable, payable and compliant. Usually part of a build; available separately as a catch-up service.

For: existing stores without SEO; payments repeatedly declined; stores missing policy pages and at risk of suspension.

What is included

  • Page title / description / URL optimization
  • Product keyword suggestions
  • PayPal / Stripe / credit-card setup
  • Privacy policy, terms of service, refund policy pages
  • Checkout flow review and fixes
06 · Maintain & Advise

Redesign · Maintenance · Advisory

Existing-store redesign, feature additions, routine maintenance, and category or market advice based on public data. Existing clients get priority scheduling.

For: stores that need upgrading; features you need but do not know who to ask; uncertainty about category or market.

What is included

  • Existing-store redesign (visual / structure / mobile)
  • Feature additions (reviews, multilingual, subscriptions, etc.)
  • Routine troubleshooting and fixes
  • Category and market advice (public data based)
  • Small proposals on request, quoted before work starts

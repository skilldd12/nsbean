Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026. Short version: we only keep what you send us, and we never sell it.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information you choose to send us: your name, contact email, product category, target market, and anything you describe about your project in an email or inquiry form.

This site itself does not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts. Your browser may request fonts and standard web assets, but we do not use this site to profile you.

2. How we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a plan or quote
  • To communicate about your project during delivery
  • To keep a record of agreed scope and commitments

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your information
  • We do not add you to marketing lists without consent
  • We do not store your payment details; payments for platforms are made by you directly

4. How long we keep it

Project-related emails and records are kept while the project relationship is active and for a reasonable period afterward for service continuity. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

5. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the information you sent us, at any time, by emailing 2096243628@qq.com. We respond within 7 days.

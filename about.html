Journey · our road

Eight years, one store at a time

From selling ourselves to helping others sell overseas. The path is simple: become operators first, then turn the methodology into a service.

2018
Started as marketplace sellers

Ran Amazon and eBay stores, learning sourcing, logistics and support the hard way.

2020
Moved to DTC stores

Built and operated brand stores, studying traffic, conversion and ad models beyond a single marketplace.

2022
Formed a service team

Turned build, design, ads and operations into repeatable methods and started serving other merchants.

2024
Adopted an AI toolchain

Rebuilt our build and content pipeline with AI, cutting delivery from weeks to 3 days and spending the saved time on detail.

Today
Focused on store builds and operations

One thing only: helping sellers open, run and profit from their own stores.

FAQ · questions you may have

Everything you might ask

How long does it take?

A basic store is delivered in 3 days; a full package with design, ads and operations takes about 5-7 days. Rush orders can be arranged, but never at the cost of quality.

How do payments work? How much upfront?

After the plan is confirmed, 50% upfront to start, balance on delivery and acceptance. Platform subscription and domain costs are paid by you directly to the provider; we never handle that money.

I am not technical. Can I still run it?

Yes. Delivery includes a guide plus a live walkthrough. If you prefer, choose managed operations and we run the daily work.

Can I see the result before deciding?

Yes. Every case on the case studies page is a live site — open them directly. We can also draft the homepage design before you commit.

Which platform do you build on?

Shopify when a hosted store with built-in checkout and shipping fits best; WordPress/WooCommerce when you want full ownership and no platform lock-in; or a static site with inquiry forms for B2B. We recommend based on your category and budget, and you pay platform fees directly.

Can you help with product selection or market advice?

Yes. Based on your sourcing and budget, we give direction on category, pricing reference and target market as part of the store plan, at no extra charge.

Do you run ads too?

We produce ad creative and advise on structure. If you want managed ad accounts, we first review your account situation, then quote based on actual workload.

What if I need features added later?

Just ask. Features are quoted individually, and existing clients get priority scheduling.

What if I am not happy with the result?

There is a confirmation step before delivery; we build to the confirmed draft. After delivery, small fixes are free within 7 days. Our principle is to align expectations first, not "find out at the end".

Can we talk on WhatsApp or WeChat?

Email is our primary channel (2096243628@qq.com): stable and traceable. After you write, we can arrange a call or chat in the email thread if needed.
